Generalized Whittaker Models and Wavefront Sets under Depth Reduction

Posted November 27, 2026 · 70 pages

Download PDF

Abstract

For the Dat–Fintzen equivalence from depth-zero representations to a rational sector of a tame \(p\)-adic group, an integral cellular kernel on the source building computes the target generalized Whittaker model with its Hecke and neutral-centralizer actions. In sufficiently large residue characteristic, a single compact character projector computes each maximal rational model on the entire corresponding quotient category. Trace ideals then give canonical rational wavefront strata and an algebra for each maximal layer. If the source depth-zero character expansion holds on the full positive-depth domain, a finite cellular trace formula transfers all local character germs under the actual equivalence. When the target germ is given by shifted orbital integrals, the cones of maximal source orbits bound its rational wavefront set. For a rational parabolic Levi, the full transfer matrix counts rational flags in generalized Springer fibres and gives equality at the original depths. For a general twisted Levi, equality holds uniformly after sufficiently deep square dilation of a matched datum. For general linear groups, compatibility with Jacquet and Whittaker functors gives a weighted partition formula and a comparison of maximal models in families.
