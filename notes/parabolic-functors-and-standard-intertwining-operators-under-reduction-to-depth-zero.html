RESEARCH NOTE

Parabolic functors and standard intertwining operators under reduction to depth zero

Posted November 27, 2026 · 53 pages

Download PDF

Abstract

We compare parabolic induction, Jacquet restriction and standard intertwining operators under the Dat–Fintzen equivalences. The Jacquet quotients construct coefficient systems on compatible Levi subgroups, with explicit identifications for different parabolics and for nested Levis. For a Levi given by the centralizer of the split center of its depth-zero partner, the two adjunctions give the same induction map over every algebra over the fixed integral coefficient ring. The universal standard operators and Harish-Chandra \(j\)-elements consequently agree over Noetherian coefficient rings. Over the complex numbers we obtain a comparison of local coefficient divisors, analytic \(R\)-groups and Deligne–Lusztig duality.
