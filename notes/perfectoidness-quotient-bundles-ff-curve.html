MATHEMATICAL NOTE

Perfectoidness of moduli spaces of quotient bundles on the FF curve

Jiawei Yang

Note · Posted on this website: September 28, 2026 · 5 pages

Download PDF

Abstract

Let F/ℚₚ be a finite extension and let C be a fixed complete algebraically closed field containing F̆. For 0 < d < n, let Wn,d denote the v-sheaf parametrizing quotient bundles 𝒪(1/n) ↠ ℰ on the relative Fargues–Fontaine curve whose geometric fibres are isomorphic to 𝒪(1/d). We prove that Wn,d is represented by a perfectoid space. This is Conjecture 5.4 in [HM].
