RESEARCH NOTE

Picard groups of principal-series Bernstein centers

Posted November 27, 2026 · 16 pages

Download PDF

Abstract

We prove that tame principal-series Bernstein centers of split groups with absolutely simple derived group have trivial Picard group. If the residue characteristic is prime to the Weyl-group order, the conclusion holds at every depth. For central quotients of products of type \(A\), we give a formula on the character lattice and use it to realize every finite abelian group. Over the algebraic integers with \(p\) inverted, we prove Picard vanishing for affine torus quotients by finite groups of order prime to \(p\). For a finitely generated abelian group, its group algebra and its invariants under a finite group of order prime to \(p\) have no \(p\)-primary Picard torsion. We construct finite free Laurent orders with classes of arbitrary prescribed order which become trivial on every geometric coefficient fiber.
