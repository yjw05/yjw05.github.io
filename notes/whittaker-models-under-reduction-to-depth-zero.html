RESEARCH NOTE

Whittaker models under reduction to depth zero

Posted November 27, 2026 · 51 pages

Download PDF

Abstract

Let \(G\) be a tamely split quasi-split group over a \(p\)-adic field. Under an explicit bound on \(p\), a Whittaker datum selects a unique rational sector of the Dat–Fintzen depth-reduction equivalence. On that sector we identify the Gelfand–Graev projection over \(\overline{\mathbb Z}[1/p]\) with the image of the depth-zero Gelfand–Graev representation of the source. A choice of this isomorphism gives a comparison of Whittaker quotients after arbitrary extension of coefficients. Regular nilpotent character coefficients identify the source datum. Compact tests give an isomorphism after taking \(p^s\) copies of each representing object, and the Rodier involution gives a perfect pairing on their comparison line. Together they give a generator of the line.
