Dense Non-Archimedean Entire Curves on Kummer Surfaces

Jiawei Yang

Preprint · First posted: October 6, 2026 · 5 pages

Download PDF

Abstract

For every prime p, we construct a smooth projective Kummer K3 surface over Cp admitting a Zariski-dense analytic map from the entire analytic line. The construction gives counterexamples to Garcia-Fritz and Pasten's conjecture [GFP25, Conjecture 1.8], since these surfaces have Kodaira dimension zero.
