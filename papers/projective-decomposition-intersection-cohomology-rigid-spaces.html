Projective Decomposition and Intersection Cohomology of Rigid Spaces

Jiawei Yang

Preprint · First posted: October 15, 2026 · 30 pages

Download PDF

Abstract

We prove the decomposition theorem for projective morphisms of rigid analytic spaces over p-adic fields, with ℚℓ-coefficients for every prime ℓ, including ℓ = p. The proof combines simultaneous algebraic approximation with a controlled restriction of perverse sheaves along analytic graphs, using vanishing cycles, Gysin duality, and comparison between coefficient primes. As an application, we show that the p-adic intersection cohomology of every proper rigid analytic space is de Rham. We also establish an independent criterion for de Rham intersection cohomology with local coefficients in terms of the dimension of the boundary, yielding further results for curves, surfaces, and finite covers.
